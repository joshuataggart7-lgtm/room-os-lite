## Sounds made for Lite

sfx_cowbells, sfx_cowbell_single, bed_road and sting_fanclub are original procedural audio from scripts/synth-lite.py (no samples). radio_hype is the Room OS line narr_hype_generic run through a radio filter.

## Sounds

Crowd recordings: AF Crowd Cheer Nat End .wav by mglennsound, March 13, 2023.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/mglennsound/sounds/678546/
License: CC0 1.0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Public HQ preview was converted to PCM WAV, trimmed, faded and loudness-normalized (normalize.sh, Oct 2026).
Derived files: stadium_cheer.mp3, stadium_bed.mp3, crowd_roar.mp3, big_play.mp3 (WAV until v7; converted to MP3 VBR q2 in v7 to keep the zip under 25 MB).
This is a generic basketball crowd, not a recording of Petco Park or any specific team.
Other built-in WAV sounds are the project's generated effects. Custom uploads belong to their respective owners.

Phase 2 (Oct 2026):
- pa_*.mp3 (announcer lines) and chant_*.mp3 (crowd chants): generated with ElevenLabs text-to-speech using
  premade library voices under the account's ElevenLabs plan. Generic arena voice; not modeled on any real
  announcer or person. Chants layer several premade voices over the CC0 crowd bed above. Manifest:
  ../assets/elevenlabs-manifest.json. Log (no key): ../assets/elevenlabs-generated.json.
- sfx_*.mp3 and mus_*.mp3: original procedural audio made by scripts/synth-sfx.mjs (additive tones, noise,
  ffmpeg filters). Crowd beds, roars and the tension murmur are derived from the CC0 recording above.
  The ship's whistle is a synthesized stand-in, not the Petco Park recording.
  mus_take_me_out_organ: original organ arrangement of "Take Me Out to the Ball Game" (Albert Von Tilzer and
  Jack Norworth, 1908, public domain), mixed over the CC0 crowd.
- Spotify songs are streamed by your own Spotify app; no song audio is bundled.

v5 (Oct 2026):
- Real sound effects generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (text prompts in ../assets/elevenlabs-manifest.json,
  log in ../assets/elevenlabs-generated.json) under the account's ElevenLabs plan, replacing the procedural
  stand-ins: sfx_roar_football, sfx_roar_baseball, sfx_roar_basketball, sfx_roar_huge, sfx_crowd_build,
  sfx_tension_murmur, sfx_bed_football, sfx_bed_baseball, sfx_bed_arena, sfx_bed_dome, sfx_grove_tailgate,
  sfx_concourse, sfx_morning, sfx_band_distant, sfx_ships_whistle (a generic ship's horn, not the Petco Park
  recording), sfx_organ_charge, sfx_organ_lets_go, sfx_musket_volley, sfx_horse_gallop, sfx_metal_stomp,
  sfx_goal_horn, sfx_drumline, sfx_brass_fanfare, sfx_fireworks, sfx_fireworks_show. Prompts describe generic
  sounds and original melodies only; no team's recording or copyrighted song was requested.
- stands_bed_*.mp3: 40 s loops mixed by scripts/synth-sfx.mjs from the generated beds above, the generated
  stands_murmur and (plaza) the procedural sfx_second_line groove.
- vend_*.mp3, narr_*.mp3, the new pa_*.mp3, stands_murmur and chant_hotty_toddy_reply: ElevenLabs
  text-to-speech with premade library voices. The "Are you ready?" narrator is a generic voice, not any
  celebrity or real announcer.

  sfx_second_line, sfx_musket_single, sfx_bell_toll, sfx_buzzer, mus_*.
